Roofing material calculator

This calculator is designed around common US roofing measurements. Enter building length and width in feet, use a pitch multiplier to approximate the sloped roof surface, add a waste percentage and enter the number of bundles required per roofing square.

Roofing square formula

One roofing square equals 100 square feet of roof area.

Base area = Length × Width
Sloped area = Base area × Pitch multiplier
Area with waste = Sloped area × (1 + Waste %)
Roofing squares = Area with waste ÷ 100

Example

A 50 ft × 30 ft footprint with a 1.12 pitch multiplier gives about 1,680 sq ft of roof surface. With 10% waste, that becomes 1,848 sq ft or 18.48 roofing squares. At 3 bundles per square, the mathematical requirement is 55.44 bundles, so purchasing would normally require rounding up to whole bundles.

Why actual material can differ

Valleys, hips, ridges, dormers, starter strips, cap shingles, flashing and unusual roof geometry can increase material needs. The pitch multiplier must also match the roof slope. Use measured roof planes for a more precise takeoff.

Common questions

What waste percentage should I use?

Waste depends on roof complexity and material. Use a percentage appropriate to the specific job rather than treating the default as a recommendation.

Does this work in metric units?

This version is intentionally US-focused and uses feet, square feet and roofing squares.
