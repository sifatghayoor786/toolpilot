Break-even point calculator

The break-even point is the volume at which total contribution covers fixed costs, before profit begins. Enter fixed costs, selling price per unit and variable cost per unit.

Formula

Contribution per unit = Selling price − Variable cost
Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution per unit
Break-even revenue = Break-even units × Selling price

Example

With $25,000 of fixed costs, a $25 selling price and $14 variable cost per unit, contribution is $11 per unit. The mathematical break-even volume is about 2,272.73 units, so whole-unit planning would require 2,273 units. Break-even revenue is about $56,818.18 before rounding units.

Why contribution matters

If selling price is less than or equal to variable cost, each additional unit does not contribute positively toward fixed costs. In that situation a conventional break-even volume is not meaningful.

Common questions

Are salaries fixed or variable costs?

It depends on how the cost behaves in your business. Classify costs based on whether they change with the relevant activity level.

Does break-even include tax?

Not automatically. Use figures that match the costing basis you intend to analyze.
