Pay raise calculator

Enter your current annual salary and the raise percentage to calculate the new annual salary, increase per year and average increase per month.

Formula

Annual increase = Current salary × Raise %
New salary = Current salary + Annual increase
Monthly increase = Annual increase ÷ 12

Example

A $65,000 salary with a 5% raise increases by $3,250 per year, producing a new annual salary of $68,250. The average monthly increase before deductions is about $270.83.

Gross pay versus take-home pay

This tool uses gross salary only. Taxes, pension contributions, insurance, bonuses and other payroll deductions can change the amount that reaches your bank account.

Common questions

Does the monthly increase match every payslip?

Not necessarily. Pay frequency, withholding and payroll adjustments can make actual payslip changes differ.

Can I use it for a salary decrease?

The tool is intended for positive raise percentages; negative values should be interpreted carefully.
