Markup and margin calculator

Enter unit cost and your intended markup percentage to calculate selling price, profit per unit and the resulting gross margin.

Formula

Selling price = Cost × (1 + Markup %)
Unit profit = Selling price − Cost
Gross margin % = Unit profit ÷ Selling price × 100

Example

At an $80 unit cost and 25% markup, selling price is $100 and unit profit is $20. Because margin is measured against selling price, the resulting gross margin is 20% rather than 25%.

Markup is not margin

This distinction matters in pricing. A business targeting a specific gross margin should not simply enter the same percentage as markup because the two percentages use different bases.

Common questions

What markup gives a 25% margin?

A 25% gross margin corresponds to a markup of about 33.33% on cost.

Does this include tax?

No. The calculation focuses on cost, selling price and gross profit before any separately applied sales tax or VAT.
