Overtime pay calculator

Enter total hours worked, your regular hourly rate, the hour threshold after which overtime begins and the applicable overtime multiplier. The tool separates regular hours from overtime hours and estimates total pay.

Formula

Regular hours = lesser of Hours worked or Threshold
Overtime hours = Hours worked − Threshold
Regular pay = Regular hours × Hourly rate
Overtime pay = Overtime hours × Hourly rate × Multiplier

Example

At $25/hour for 48 hours, with overtime beginning after 40 hours at a 1.5× multiplier, regular pay is $1,000, overtime pay is $300 and modeled total pay is $1,300.

Use the rules that apply to you

Overtime eligibility, thresholds, workweek definitions, exemptions, premiums and treatment of bonuses vary by jurisdiction and employment classification. The default values are examples only, so enter the threshold and multiplier applicable to your situation.

Common questions

Does the calculator determine whether I am legally entitled to overtime?

No. It only applies the numbers you enter.

Can I use a multiplier other than 1.5?

Yes. The multiplier is configurable.
