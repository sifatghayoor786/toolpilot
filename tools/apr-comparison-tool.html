APR loan comparison calculator

Use this tool to compare two fixed-rate amortizing loan offers for the same principal. Enter each loan's APR and term to see the estimated monthly payment and total amount repaid.

How the comparison works

Each option uses the standard fixed-payment amortization formula. The calculator then compares total repayment across the full term.

Example

For a $30,000 loan, Option A at 6.5% APR over 5 years is about $586.98 per month with total repayment around $35,219.07. Option B at 8% APR over 4 years is about $732.39 per month with total repayment around $35,154.61. The total-cost difference is only about $64.46, showing why APR and term should be considered together.

Important limitation

This simplified comparison does not separately add origination fees, prepayment penalties, insurance or other lender charges. If those costs are already embedded in a disclosed APR, do not add them again without checking the lender's methodology.

Common questions

Can a higher APR loan cost less overall?

Yes, if its term is materially shorter. A higher rate over fewer months can sometimes produce a lower total repayment.

Does the lower monthly payment always mean the cheaper loan?

No. A longer term can reduce the monthly payment while increasing total interest paid.
