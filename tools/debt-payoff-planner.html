Debt Payoff Planner

Estimate how long a fixed monthly payment could take to repay a balance and how much interest may accrue.

Enter your values and run the tool.

Debt payoff calculator

Enter a debt balance, annual percentage rate and fixed monthly payment. The calculator applies monthly interest to the remaining balance and then subtracts the payment, repeating the process until the modeled balance reaches zero.

How the simulation works

Monthly rate = APR ÷ 12
Monthly interest = Current balance × Monthly rate
New balance = Current balance + Interest − Payment

Example

A $12,000 balance at 19% APR with a fixed $450 monthly payment takes about 35 months in this model and produces approximately $3,714.19 of interest, assuming the rate and payment stay unchanged.

Payment-too-low warning

If the payment does not exceed the interest generated in a month, the balance will not amortize under this simplified model. The tool flags that condition instead of showing a misleading payoff date.

What can change the real payoff

Credit-card rates can vary, fees may apply, minimum-payment rules can change, interest can be calculated using different daily methods and new purchases can increase the balance. Use current account statements for exact figures.

Common questions

Does making a larger payment reduce interest?

In a fixed-rate model, paying principal faster usually reduces the amount of future interest generated.

Does this combine multiple debts?

No. This version models one balance at a time.
