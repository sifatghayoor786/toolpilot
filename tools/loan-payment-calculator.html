Loan repayment calculator

Use this tool to estimate the payment on a fixed-rate amortizing loan. Enter the amount borrowed, annual percentage rate (APR) and term in years to see the estimated monthly payment, total interest and total amount repaid.

Formula used

The calculator uses the standard fixed-payment amortization formula, where the monthly rate is the annual rate divided by 12 and the number of payments is the term in years multiplied by 12.

Example

A $25,000 loan at 7.5% APR over 5 years produces an estimated monthly payment of about $500.95. Across 60 payments, total repayment is about $30,056.92, including roughly $5,056.92 of interest.

What the result does not include

Real loans may include origination fees, insurance, taxes, variable rates, balloon payments, promotional periods or lender-specific rounding. APR definitions and disclosure rules also differ by market. Use the lender's official disclosure for a final decision.

Common questions

What happens if the APR is 0%?

The calculator divides the principal evenly across the selected number of monthly payments.

Can I use it for car, personal or business loans?

Yes, when the loan uses a conventional fixed-rate amortizing structure. It is not designed for credit cards, interest-only products or irregular payment schedules.

Does currency change the formula?

No. Currency changes the display only; the amortization math is the same.
