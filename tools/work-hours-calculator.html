Work Hours Calculator

Calculate paid daily and total work hours after subtracting unpaid breaks.

Enter your values and run the tool.

Work hours calculator

Enter the number of days worked, start hour, end hour and unpaid break minutes per day. The calculator subtracts the break and multiplies the paid daily hours by the number of days.

Formula

Paid hours per day = End time − Start time − Break minutes ÷ 60
Total paid hours = Paid hours per day × Days worked

Example

Working from 9:00 to 17:30 gives 8.5 elapsed hours. After a 30-minute unpaid break, paid time is 8 hours per day. Across 5 days, total paid time is 40 hours.

Input format

This version uses decimal 24-hour values. For example, 17.5 represents 17:30. It is best suited to same-day shifts where the end time is later than the start time.

Common questions

Does it support overnight shifts?

Not in this simple version. Overnight shifts need a calculation that crosses midnight.

Does it decide whether a break should be paid?

No. Enter only the unpaid break duration that applies to your situation.
