Resume and job-description keyword checker

Paste your resume and the target job description to compare recurring terms. The checker extracts prominent words from the job description, then shows which of those terms also appear in your resume and which may be missing.

How the score works

The overlap percentage is based on a limited set of recurring job-description terms after common filler words are removed. It is a text-comparison aid, not an ATS score and not a prediction of hiring outcomes.

How to use the result

Review missing terms one by one. Add a skill, tool or responsibility only when it truthfully reflects your experience. The strongest resume language usually connects relevant keywords to specific evidence, outcomes and context rather than repeating a job description verbatim.

What the checker cannot evaluate

It does not understand whether a claim is true, whether experience is senior enough, whether a qualification is mandatory, or how any specific employer configures recruiting software. It also does not evaluate formatting, career history or interview performance.

Common questions

Should I copy every missing keyword?

No. Include only accurate and relevant terminology. Artificial keyword stuffing can make a resume harder to read.

Is the text sent to an AI service?

No. This version performs the comparison in your browser and does not require an account.
