Overall Equipment Effectiveness calculator

OEE combines three manufacturing metrics into one measure: Availability, Performance and Quality. It is most useful when the definitions and data-collection rules stay consistent across machines, lines and reporting periods.

OEE formulas

Availability = Run time ÷ Planned production time
Performance = (Ideal cycle time × Total count) ÷ Run time
Quality = Good count ÷ Total count
OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality

Example

Using 480 planned minutes, 420 run minutes, a 2.5-second ideal cycle time, 9,000 total units and 8,700 good units gives Availability of 87.5%, Performance of about 89.29%, Quality of about 96.67% and modeled OEE of about 75.52%.

Interpret the losses separately

Two machines can have the same OEE for very different reasons. Availability points to downtime, Performance to speed loss and minor stops, and Quality to rejected output. Diagnose the three components before acting on the combined number.

Common questions

Can OEE exceed 100%?

With valid inputs and a true ideal cycle time, the components should not exceed 100%. A result above 100% usually indicates a data-definition or ideal-cycle-time issue.

Should planned breaks be included?

That depends on your OEE definition. Choose a consistent planned-production-time policy and use it across comparisons.
