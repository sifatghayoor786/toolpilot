Rent versus buy monthly-cost calculator

This tool compares current monthly rent with a simplified estimate of monthly homeownership cost based on purchase price, down payment, mortgage rate, term, property tax, insurance and maintenance.

How ownership cost is modeled

The mortgage portion uses a standard fixed-rate amortization formula. Property tax plus insurance and maintenance are estimated as annual percentages of home price and converted to monthly amounts.

Example

For a $450,000 home with 20% down, a 6% 30-year mortgage, 1.8% annual property tax plus insurance and 1% annual maintenance, modeled principal-and-interest is about $2,158.38 per month. The simplified total ownership cost is about $3,208.38 per month, compared with $2,200 rent in the default example.

Important limitations

This is not a full rent-versus-buy investment model. It does not automatically include closing costs, mortgage insurance, HOA fees, utilities, appreciation, rent inflation, tax effects, transaction costs, opportunity cost of the down payment or future selling costs.

Common questions

Does a lower monthly rent automatically mean renting is financially better?

No. Monthly cash cost is only one part of a full long-term comparison.

Does the tool include home equity?

No. It compares simplified monthly costs rather than accumulated equity or investment returns.
