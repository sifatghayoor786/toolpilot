Free invoice total calculator

Use this invoice generator when you already know the line-item subtotal and need a fast, transparent total. Choose USD, GBP, CAD, AUD or EUR, enter any discount, then add the sales tax or VAT percentage that applies to your transaction.

How the calculation works

The discount is deducted first. Tax is then calculated on the discounted subtotal.

Discount = Subtotal × Discount %
Taxable subtotal = Subtotal − Discount
Tax = Taxable subtotal × Tax %
Invoice total = Taxable subtotal + Tax

Example

For a $1,000 subtotal with a 10% discount and 8% tax, the discount is $100, the taxable subtotal is $900, tax is $72 and the final invoice total is $972.

US, UK, Canada, Australia and Europe

The calculator supports multiple currencies, but it does not choose a tax rate for you. Enter the rate required for your sale, whether that is a US sales-tax rate, VAT, GST/HST or another applicable consumption tax. Tax rules can depend on location, product type and customer status.

Common questions

Does this create a downloadable invoice?

This version calculates the financial summary. Use the result as the total for your own invoice document or billing system.

Is tax applied before or after the discount?

This tool applies tax after the discount. If your jurisdiction or contract uses a different treatment, calculate according to the rule that applies to you.

Is my invoice data uploaded?

The calculation runs in your browser and does not require an account.
