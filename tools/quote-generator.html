Job quote and estimate calculator

Use this tool to turn your direct job costs into a quick customer-facing estimate. Enter materials, labor and other direct costs, add your chosen markup, then include tax if it applies to the transaction.

How the quote is calculated

Direct cost = Materials + Labor + Other direct costs
Markup amount = Direct cost × Markup %
Pre-tax quote = Direct cost + Markup
Tax = Pre-tax quote × Tax %
Estimated quote = Pre-tax quote + Tax

Example

With $2,500 of materials, $1,800 of labor and $300 of other direct costs, direct cost is $4,600. A 20% markup adds $920, producing a $5,520 pre-tax quote.

Quote versus final invoice

A quote is usually a price proposal based on the known scope. Final invoices can change if the scope, quantities, hours or approved variations change. Clearly state what is included, excluded and how long the quote remains valid.

Tax and currency note

The calculator supports USD, GBP, CAD, AUD and EUR, but it does not determine whether tax applies or which rate is correct. Sales tax, VAT, GST and similar rules differ by location and transaction.

Common questions

Is markup the same as profit margin?

No. Markup is added to cost; margin expresses profit as a percentage of the selling price.

Should overhead be included?

If overhead is not already built into your labor rate or markup policy, account for it before treating the estimate as a sustainable selling price.
