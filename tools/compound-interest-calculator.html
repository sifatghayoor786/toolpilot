Compound interest and contribution calculator

Use this tool to model how a starting amount and regular monthly contributions could grow if they earned a constant annual rate compounded monthly. It separates the amount you contribute from the modeled investment growth.

How the projection works

The starting balance compounds monthly. Each monthly contribution is also accumulated using the same modeled monthly rate. The annual rate is divided by 12 for the calculation.

Example

Starting with $10,000, adding $500 per month for 10 years and modeling a constant 7% annual return produces a projected value of about $106,639.02. Total contributions are $70,000, leaving about $36,639.02 as modeled growth.

This is a projection, not a forecast

Real investment returns are not constant. Markets can rise or fall, fees and taxes can reduce returns, contribution timing can differ and inflation affects purchasing power. Use the result for mathematical scenario planning rather than as a promise of future performance.

Common questions

Are contributions assumed at the beginning or end of each month?

This implementation models regular monthly contributions using an ordinary-annuity structure, effectively treating contributions as occurring at the end of each monthly period.

Can the return rate be negative?

The tool is designed primarily for standard positive-rate scenarios; unusual rates should be interpreted carefully.
