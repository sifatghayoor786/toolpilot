Renovation Budget Planner

Plan a renovation budget by work category and add a separate contingency reserve.

Enter your values and calculate.

Home renovation budget planner

This planner helps you combine major renovation categories into one budget and keep contingency visible instead of hiding it inside individual line items. Enter planned amounts for kitchen, bathroom, flooring, painting and other work, then select a contingency percentage.

How the budget is calculated

Planned work = Kitchen + Bathroom + Flooring + Painting + Other work
Contingency reserve = Planned work × Contingency %
Total renovation budget = Planned work + Contingency reserve

Example

Using the default values, planned work totals $41,500. A 15% contingency reserve adds $6,225, giving a total modeled renovation budget of $47,725.

What contingency is for

Renovation projects can reveal hidden conditions, specification changes, quantity differences or price changes. A separately tracked reserve makes those uncertainties visible. The appropriate reserve depends on project scope, design completeness and property condition.

What this planner does not include automatically

Professional design fees, permits, temporary accommodation, financing costs, taxes, appliances, furniture and owner-supplied items may need separate lines depending on the project.

Common questions

Should I spend the contingency from day one?

A contingency is generally more useful as a reserve for uncertainty than as part of the base scope.

Can I use pounds, Canadian dollars or euros?

Yes. Currency changes the display; the budget math is the same.
