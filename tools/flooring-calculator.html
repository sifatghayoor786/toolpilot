Flooring material calculator

Enter room length and width in feet to calculate square footage, then add a waste allowance for cuts, offcuts, pattern matching and unusable pieces. Add your material price per square foot to estimate the purchase cost.

Formula

Room area = Length × Width
Material to buy = Room area × (1 + Waste %)
Estimated material cost = Material to buy × Price per ft²

Example

A 20 ft × 15 ft room is 300 ft². With 10% waste, the planned purchase quantity becomes 330 ft². At $4.25 per ft², estimated material cost is $1,402.50.

Why waste allowance varies

Simple rectangular rooms can require less cutting than diagonal layouts, patterned tile, herringbone, multiple rooms or irregular spaces. Packaging quantities can also force you to buy more than the mathematical result.

Common questions

Does this include installation labor?

No. The displayed cost is for material based on the price per square foot entered.

Does this support metric units?

This version is targeted to US-style feet and square feet.
