Salary to hourly converter

Use this calculator to translate an annual salary into monthly, weekly and hourly equivalents. Enter the annual salary, expected hours per week and number of working weeks per year.

Formula

Monthly pay = Annual salary ÷ 12
Weekly pay = Annual salary ÷ Working weeks
Hourly pay = Annual salary ÷ (Hours per week × Working weeks)

Example

A $70,000 annual salary over 52 working weeks and 40 hours per week is about $5,833.33 per month, $1,346.15 per week and $33.65 per hour before deductions.

Why working weeks matter

If you use fewer than 52 working weeks, the implied weekly and hourly equivalents increase because the same annual salary is spread across fewer working periods. Use the convention that best matches the comparison you are making.

Common questions

Does this include paid leave?

No special adjustment is made. The result depends entirely on the working weeks and hours you enter.

Is hourly equivalent the same as an employee's legal hourly rate?

Not always. Payroll, overtime and employment-law definitions can use different rules.
