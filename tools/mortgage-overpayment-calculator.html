Mortgage extra-payment calculator

Regular overpayments reduce principal faster, which can reduce the interest charged in later months. This calculator compares the standard remaining mortgage schedule with a schedule that includes a fixed extra payment every month.

How it works

The standard monthly payment is calculated from the current balance, interest rate and remaining term. The tool then simulates the balance month by month, first with the normal payment and then with your chosen extra payment.

Example

On a $300,000 balance at 6% with 25 years remaining, the standard payment is about $1,932.90 per month. Adding $250 per month can reduce the modeled payoff period by about 67 months and reduce modeled interest by roughly $71,272, assuming the rate stays fixed and there are no prepayment charges.

Before making an overpayment

Mortgage contracts vary. Some lenders allow unrestricted extra principal payments, while others impose annual limits, early-repayment charges or special instructions for applying extra money to principal. Check the terms of your mortgage before relying on an estimate.

Common questions

Does the calculator assume the interest rate stays fixed?

Yes. If your mortgage rate changes, actual savings will differ.

Does an overpayment always shorten the term?

In this model, extra money is applied to principal while the scheduled payment continues, which shortens the payoff period.
