Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026.

ToolPilot does not require an account. Values entered into the calculators and generators are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally sent to ToolPilot for calculation.

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages, so technical request data may be processed by the hosting provider under its own policies. ToolPilot currently does not run display advertising or a third-party analytics service. If advertising, analytics, cookies or consent-management services are added, this policy will be updated to describe those services before activation.

Do not enter confidential or sensitive information into tools unless you are comfortable processing it on your own device.
