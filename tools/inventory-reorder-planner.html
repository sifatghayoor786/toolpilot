Inventory reorder point calculator

A reorder point is the stock level at which replenishment should be triggered so expected demand can be covered while the next order is in transit. This simplified planner uses average daily demand, lead time and a safety-stock allowance expressed in days.

Formula

Lead-time demand = Average daily demand × Lead time
Safety stock = Average daily demand × Safety-stock days
Reorder point = Lead-time demand + Safety stock

Example

At 120 units of average daily demand, a 14-day lead time requires 1,680 units for expected lead-time demand. Five safety-stock days add 600 units, producing a reorder point of 2,280 units.

When this simple model works best

The method is useful when demand and lead time are reasonably stable. Highly seasonal products, intermittent demand, uncertain supplier performance or minimum-order constraints may require a more advanced safety-stock method.

Common questions

Is safety stock the same as reorder point?

No. Safety stock is the buffer. The reorder point combines expected lead-time demand with that buffer.

Should stock on open purchase orders be included?

For operational decisions, many businesses evaluate inventory position rather than only physical on-hand stock. Define your policy consistently before using the result.
