Gross profit calculator

Gross profit shows how much revenue remains after subtracting cost of goods sold (COGS). Gross margin expresses that gross profit as a percentage of revenue.

Formula

Gross profit = Revenue − COGS
Gross margin % = Gross profit ÷ Revenue × 100

Example

With $125,000 of revenue and $78,000 of COGS, gross profit is $47,000 and gross margin is 37.6%.

What belongs in COGS?

COGS depends on the business model and accounting method. For product businesses it commonly includes costs directly associated with goods sold. Operating expenses such as office administration or marketing are generally analyzed separately from gross profit.

Common questions

Is gross profit the same as net profit?

No. Net profit also reflects operating expenses and other items beyond COGS.

Can gross margin be negative?

Yes. If COGS exceeds revenue, gross profit and gross margin are negative.
