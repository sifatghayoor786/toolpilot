Savings goal planner

Enter your target amount, current savings, expected annual return and time horizon. The calculator first projects the current balance forward, then estimates the monthly contribution required to close the remaining gap.

How the calculation works

The existing balance compounds monthly at the modeled rate. Required monthly savings are then calculated as a series of end-of-month contributions using the same modeled monthly rate.

Example

With a $50,000 target, $10,000 already saved, a 4% annual modeled return and 3 years remaining, the current balance grows to about $11,272.72. The modeled monthly contribution needed is about $1,014.29.

This is a planning scenario

Actual savings-account or investment returns can change, and fees or taxes may apply. If your savings product has a variable rate, the final required contribution may differ.

Common questions

What if my current savings already reach the target?

The required monthly contribution falls to zero in this simplified model.

Are monthly contributions assumed at the start of the month?

No. This implementation models contributions at the end of each monthly period.
