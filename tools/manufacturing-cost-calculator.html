Manufacturing cost per unit calculator

This tool provides a compact factory-cost view by combining direct material, direct labor, manufacturing overhead and scrap or waste cost, then dividing the total by good units produced.

Formula

Total manufacturing cost = Materials + Direct labor + Manufacturing overhead + Scrap/Waste cost
Cost per good unit = Total manufacturing cost ÷ Good units produced

Example

With $12,000 materials, $5,000 direct labor, $3,500 manufacturing overhead and $800 scrap cost, total modeled manufacturing cost is $21,300. At 100,000 good units, the modeled cost is $0.213 per good unit.

What belongs in manufacturing overhead?

Depending on your costing method, factory overhead may include indirect production labor, utilities, depreciation, maintenance, supervision and other production-support expenses. Selling, distribution and corporate administrative costs are often tracked separately.

Good output matters

Dividing by good units rather than gross production makes quality loss visible in unit cost. Use consistent definitions for scrap, rework and accepted output when comparing periods or lines.

Common questions

Is this the same as full product profitability?

No. It estimates manufacturing cost. Freight, warehousing, selling expenses, commissions, financing and taxes may need separate treatment.

Can I use kilograms or batches instead of pieces?

Yes, if the output quantity and cost basis are consistent. The result is cost per output unit entered.
