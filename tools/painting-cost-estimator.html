Estimate paint quantity, paint cost, labor and total project cost for wall painting.

Enter your values and run the tool.

Wall painting cost estimator

Enter paintable wall area, number of coats, expected paint coverage, price per gallon and labor cost per square foot. The calculator rounds paint quantity up to whole gallons and combines material and labor estimates.

Formula

Gallons = Ceiling of (Area × Coats ÷ Coverage per gallon)
Paint cost = Gallons × Price per gallon
Labor estimate = Paintable area × Labor rate per ft²

Example

For 1,800 ft² of paintable wall area, 2 coats and 350 ft² of coverage per gallon, the mathematical requirement is just over 10 gallons, so the tool rounds to 11 gallons. At $45 per gallon, paint cost is $495. At $1.25 labor per ft², modeled labor is $2,250, for a total of $2,745.

Why actual coverage changes

Porous surfaces, dramatic color changes, primer, texture, overspray, application method and product specifications can all change paint usage. Doors, trim, ceilings, repairs and prep work can also add cost.

Common questions

Does the labor rate multiply by the number of coats?

Not in this simple model. Enter a labor rate per square foot that reflects the scope you want to estimate.

Does this include primer?

No. Add primer separately if the job requires it.
