Concrete Calculator

Calculate slab concrete volume in cubic yards, add waste allowance and estimate material cost.

Enter your values and run the tool.

Concrete slab calculator

Enter slab length and width in feet, depth in inches, a waste allowance and your local concrete price per cubic yard. The calculator converts depth to feet, calculates cubic feet, converts that volume to cubic yards and then applies the waste allowance.

Formula

Cubic feet = Length × Width × (Depth in inches ÷ 12)
Base cubic yards = Cubic feet ÷ 27
Order volume = Base cubic yards × (1 + Waste %)

Example

A 20 ft × 10 ft slab at 4 inches deep has about 66.67 ft³ of concrete before waste. With 10% waste, modeled order volume is about 2.72 yd³. At $155 per cubic yard, modeled concrete cost is about $420.99.

Ordering considerations

Ready-mix suppliers may have minimum loads, short-load charges, delivery fees and ordering increments. Excavation variation and subgrade conditions can also change actual volume, so confirm field dimensions before ordering.

Common questions

Why divide cubic feet by 27?

One cubic yard contains 27 cubic feet.

Does this calculate bags of premixed concrete?

No. This version estimates bulk volume in cubic yards.
